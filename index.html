yt-stats-claude

yt-stats-claude is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel @beanjahmean to manage that one channel:

  • read the channel's own video statistics (views, watch time, subscribers gained) to see which videos work;
  • upload the channel's own finished videos with their titles, descriptions, tags and publish times.

It is not offered, sold or distributed to anyone else, and it only signs in to the owner's own Google account.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google handles data under the Google Privacy Policy.
